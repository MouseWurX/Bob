# Bob 1.5.153

The Computer Science Lab: cut recipes, a cost-and-time table before anything runs, and the usage map that shows which experts a brain uses for what. All free, on this replicant matrix; nothing is rented.

- **Cut recipes** (above the template picker). Pick where the new brain will run (this replicant matrix, a 24, 16, 12, 8 or 6 GB card, a 16 GB handheld, or a phone with 12 or 8 GB) and a goal: **Everything, smaller**, **Coding helper**, **Everyday chat**, **Game master** or **Tiny and fast**. I fill in steps 2 to 5: the number of experts that fits the device, the skills to protect at 99%, a Q4 running file (Q3 for devices with 8 GB or less), measuring before the running file, Try it first when Brain Engine is here, and the comparison test. Nothing starts until you press Start. **Fold instead of drop (Garfield)** is listed and greyed until it's built; **Just measure** opens the usage map.
- **What it takes.** A table before every Lab job: how long it takes here, from the brain's size the first time and from your own past Lab jobs after that, and the space it needs. For a cut, a second column shows what teaching it back would take on a rented card (hours, dollars and the cap), from the measured rentals: about 0.6 hours per 1,000 lessons at Riker's size, 2.5 hours for the teacher's scores, at about $5 an hour for a 141 GB card. The Lab can't rent yet; Show the recipe prints that part as a Forge plan. Every step now records how long it took.
- **The usage map** (under Compare). Count which experts a brain uses for each thing you test it on: the four parts of the Lab test, your chats with Bob, a lesson set (one count for each skill), or a file or folder of your own text and code. It lists the cold experts (never used for anything tested), the faint ones (the last 5% of the work), the ones only one thing uses, a per-layer table, and how much of each thing a cut keeps at a few sizes. With Brain Engine, it can switch the cold and faint ones off and give the Lab test again. The counts are saved as `peek-counts.json` and a keep list, in the format the Lab's Peek inside will share; the recipes build their cut from them when they're there.
- Help: a new page, Cut recipes, the estimate and the usage map. `test/run-lab-recipes-test.js`: new checks.
- Phone (Bob for Android): nothing changes; there's no Lab on the phone.
