# Bob 1.5.200

FNRI can colour a brain by its own weights now, and show what changed between two brains.

## New
- **Weight map.** In FNRI's viewer, **Colour by** has **How strong each expert's weights are**: every block coloured from dark blue (weaker than the rest of its floor) to yellow (stronger), read straight from the brain file. The glow still shows what the brain used, so you can watch thinking run over the weights.
- **Difference map.** **How much each expert differs from another brain** colours every block by how far its weights moved from a brain you pick. Pick the one it was made from: a cut brain lines up with it through its keep list, so you see what healing changed and where.
- Both read a few rows of every block from the files, with no engine and no graphics card, and change nothing. A big brain takes about a minute the first time; after that the map is kept beside the scans. They read Q2_K to Q8_0, F16 and BF16 files (not the IQ kinds yet), and work on expert and neuron scans.

Where it works: Windows, Mac, Linux, wherever the Lab runs. Android's Bob doesn't have the Lab.
