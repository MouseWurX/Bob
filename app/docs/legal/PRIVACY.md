# Privacy policy

Bob of The Idle Process runs on your own PC.

In the app, Bob calls your computer his replicant matrix.

This program will not transfer any information to other networked systems unless specifically requested by the user or the person installing or operating it.

- **What Bob collects:** nothing. Bob has no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry.
- **Where your data stays:** chats, notes, settings and downloaded models stay in folders on your PC (see "Where Bob keeps things" in the README). You can delete them at any time, or use Remove Bob in Settings.
- **When Bob goes online:**
  - **Web searches:** off unless you turn it on. With **Let Bob search the web** on (Settings), when a question needs it Bob may search the web (DuckDuckGo) and read pages on his own, without asking each time. The search words and the page address go to that site. Nothing else from your chats is sent. With it off, Bob doesn't search or open web pages at all.
  - **Security look-ups:** only with the **Security and privacy** Hand on and **Let Bob search the web** on. The website, link, email domain or internet address you ask Bob to check goes to public record services (rdap.org, crt.sh, Have I Been Pwned's breach list) and to DNS. Following a link's redirects (only when you ask) contacts that link's sites. Bob never sends your email address or files anywhere for these checks. **Check a password safely** sends only the first 5 characters of the password's SHA-1 scramble to Have I Been Pwned (api.pwnedpasswords.com); the password itself never leaves Bob's window and is not saved.
  - **Engine updates (on unless switched off):** with "Keep engines up to date" on (Settings > Engines), Bob asks GitHub about once a day whether Brain Engine has a new build, and downloads it (from github.com/MouseWurX/Bob) or llama.cpp's tested build (from github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp), each checked against its checksum. Nothing else is sent. Switch it off and this stops.
  - **Things you click:** model and engine downloads (from the address shown before you confirm), voice samples from a voice's publisher, links you click (they open in your normal web browser), "Search the web" from the right-click menu (opens DuckDuckGo in your browser with the selected words), and **Check** in the "Check a password safely" box (sends only the first 5 characters of the password's scramble to Have I Been Pwned, haveibeenpwned.com).
  - **Rented brains** (Settings, Show advanced settings, Online services) are off unless you turn a service on, and every spending limit starts at $0. With your own account and key (an environment variable Bob reads and never stores), and only after you press **Hire it** on a card, the chat of that job, the files you attached to it, Bob's instructions and what he remembers about you go to the service you picked (RunPod, OpenRouter, Together AI, Vast.ai, Lambda, or an address you typed) to be worked on there, and come under that service's privacy policy. Bob's tools still run on your PC. Vast.ai and Lambda machines take the chat over a plain, unencrypted connection. Bob keeps a ledger of what each hire cost, on your PC. Nothing goes to a service on its own.
  - **Cloud AI helpers** you switch on with your own API key (each use asks first). What you send to a cloud helper is then covered by that provider's own privacy policy.
- **Update checks:** "Check for updates" (Settings), and "Check for updates when Bob starts" if you turn it on (off to start with), ask GitHub (api.github.com) for the newest release of github.com/MouseWurX/Bob. Only that request goes out, the same as opening the releases page in a browser. "Update Bob" then downloads the release file for your computer from GitHub and checks it against its published .sha256 checksum before using it. Bob also looks at the folder he was built from on this PC, if there is one.
- **Check this PC and Test Bob's speed** (Settings) only read this PC's own details and time Bob's own brains on this PC. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Let my other replicant matrices in** (phone access) over Tailscale is off unless you turn it on.
- **Talk to Bob on Telegram** (Settings, Show advanced settings, Online services) is off unless you turn it on. You make your own bot and add its token as a Windows user variable (Bob never stores it). While it's on, Bob asks Telegram (api.telegram.org) for new messages to your bot, and your messages to the bot and Bob's replies to them pass through Telegram's servers, under Telegram's privacy policy. Card texts go there only with **Answer Bob's OK cards on Telegram** on, and pictures only with **Send pictures Bob makes** on. Nothing from your other chats is sent. Only the one Telegram account you paired can talk to Bob; messages from anyone else are ignored.
- **Bob-Net** (Settings, Show advanced settings, Bob-Net) is off unless you turn it on. With **Borrow a brain** on, the chat so far and what Bob remembers about you (with his instructions) go, for each quick reply, over Tailscale to another Bob you added yourself, which thinks the reply and sends it back. With **Lend my brain** on, this Bob does the same for your other Bobs: it doesn't save or log what they send, and nothing runs on this computer for them. With **Send jobs to my other Bobs** on, a whole job (its words and the last few lines of that chat) goes the same way to one of your Bobs that can do it; its reply, approval cards and finished files come back. With **Take jobs from my other Bobs** on, this Bob does such jobs for your other Bobs without saving them as chats; the files it makes are sent back, and a copy stays in its save folder. With **Pass my Bob-Net along** on, this Bob tells two of your other Bobs each other's name and Tailscale address so they can pair by themselves; passcodes still pass only between the two Bobs being paired, as in pairing. The Bob-Net section in Now running asks your other Bobs "are you there?" now and then while it's open, with nothing of yours attached.
- **Hands** (files, running programs, schedules, apps) are off unless you turn them on, and work only on this PC. They send nothing online by themselves; a command you approve can of course use the network (for example `git pull`), and the card shows you the exact command first. If you turn on "Let Bob work without asking", there is no card, but the "Don't send anything out" hard limit (on unless you switch it off) still blocks uploads, posts, `git push` and sending in browsers, email or chat apps. The optional "Bob schedules" Windows task only starts Bob on this PC. The Apps hand (clicking and typing in other programs) reads a window's buttons and, if you allow it, a picture of that window, only on this PC; nothing is sent anywhere. The Hands self-test runs only when you click it, on dummy files in a throwaway folder, and sends nothing online. The Connected apps (MCP) Hand talks only to apps you added and switched on yourself, and while "Don't send anything out" is on, only to apps on this computer (localhost); what an app's add-on does with a request is up to that add-on.

- **Other programs** you installed yourself and Bob can use, such as Ollama, ComfyUI or Pinokio, are separate and have their own privacy policies.
