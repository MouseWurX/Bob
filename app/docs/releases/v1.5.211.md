# Bob 1.5.211

Two fixes to the Lab's teaching pages, found in the first PC trial.

## Fixed

- **Lab, Teach it and Best answers: a brain made in the Lab can be taught again.** A brain made with Teach it or Best answers had a file that didn't say where its full weights came from. So picking it for more teaching was refused with "doesn't say where its full weights are", until you named the original on Hugging Face by hand. Bob now remembers the original each brain was made from. Picking it teaches that original again, and the plan says plainly that what the brain learned before isn't carried over. To build on the taught brain itself, teach it from a folder of its full weights. Tests in `test/run-lab-pages-test.js`.
- **Lab, Best answers: a steadier judge.** On the PC trial, a small judge (Qwen3-4B) gave one right answer 10 and another right answer 0, and gave 10 to an answer with a slip in it. Three changes:
  - The judge now reads each list of answers twice, once in order and once reversed, and the two scores are averaged.
  - Answers that are the same word for word are judged once and share a score.
  - The judge gets a plain scale: 10 for fully right, 7 for right with a slip, 4 for partly right, 1 for wrong. It is also told to give equally correct answers the same score.
  Judging takes about twice as long, which is still small next to answering. Tests in `test/run-lab-best-test.js`.

**Where it works:** everywhere the Lab's Playground works: Windows, Mac and Linux. Android and the Raspberry Pi answer and judge, and the teaching goes rented.
